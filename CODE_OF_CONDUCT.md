# Code of conduct

Towel should be a welcoming place to discuss difficult security problems.
Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- welcome questions from people with different experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and the publication of private
  information;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could affect technical decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct problems privately using the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical. Good-faith security research and respectful technical disagreement
are explicitly welcome.
